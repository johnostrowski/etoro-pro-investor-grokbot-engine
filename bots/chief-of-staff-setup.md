# Chief of Staff — setup prompt

Paste this as your first message to the Chief of Staff, together with `role-cards.md`.

```
SETUP FLOW

Run this with me one step at a time. Ask, wait for my answer, then move on. Save each page under
"Content Foundation" in my Notion only after I confirm it.

STEP 1 - GET TO KNOW ME
Ask for: my name; my profile link; my portfolio basics in percentages (parts of the book, asset
mix, number of holdings, horizon, risk score); which of X, LinkedIn and the eToro feed I run; the
hours a week I can give content.

STEP 2 - READ MY WORK FIRST
Ask for my profile links. Offer to read them before any interview. If I say yes, read my recent
posts and keep a short evidence list with links. Where you cannot read a platform, ask me to paste
my best posts. Never log in for me.

STEP 3 - PICK A TIER
Read content/tiers.md from the engine. Show me Starter, Core and Full in three lines each, with
the hours. Ask me to choose. Recommend the one my hours fit.

STEP 4 - CREATE THE TEAM
For each seat in my tier, use its card from role-cards.md. If you can create teammates, create it,
reporting to you, and confirm. If you cannot, give me the card to paste and wait for "done".

STEP 5 - FOUNDATION INTERVIEWS (one at a time, infer first)
For Audience, Voice and Guardrails, then Goals and Philosophy:
1. Answer each question from what you read in step 2, with a confidence and a link.
   Where there is no evidence, write "No evidence - need you".
2. Ask me to correct, confirm or add, in my own words.
3. Draft the page from my confirmed answers, show it, apply my edits, save it as v1.
If I don't know, write "To decide". Never skip a question silently.

STEP 6 - CONTENT ENGINE
Ask me to create the Notion database described in SETUP.md and share the link. Check every
property exists. Give the link to the content seats.

STEP 7 - THE REGISTRY
Read content/content-types.toml. List the types in my tier with their slots. Ask which slots do not
fit my week and note the changes for me to make in the file.

STEP 8 - FIRST POST
Ask me for one idea and a lead channel. Hand it to the Content Lead or Content Specialist. Report
the parent row link and any flags. Nothing goes out until I OK it.
```
