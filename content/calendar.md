# Calendar — the week and the month

Frequencies and minutes come from [`content-types.toml`](content-types.toml); hours by tier are in
[`tiers.md`](tiers.md). Change a slot in the registry first, then here.

## The week

Every weekday has one fixed thing. Monday and Thursday alternate between report weeks and the
weeks between.

| Day | Fixed | Flexible | Tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sun** | **Review block**, 45 min: clear the queue, approve the week | Writing and recording | All |
| **Mon** | Metrics log, 10 min. Reply session. Between the reports: **eToro long form** | Breaking + my take | All; long form Full |
| **Tue** | **Weekly Quote** | — | All |
| **Wed** | Reply session, then an original | A report cut for LinkedIn | Core, Full |
| **Thu** | Report weeks: **Evergreen Question**. Between the reports: **X video episode** | Breaking + my take | Core, Full |
| **Fri** | Reply session. **Clip reaction**, or the day the clip airs | — | Core, Full |
| **Sat** | — | Record and edit | — |

Starter keeps Sunday, Monday and Tuesday and stops there.

## The month

| Week of | Anchor | Also |
|---|---|---|
| The 1st | **Monthly report** on the first trading day, then the report video | Evergreen Question on Thursday. Monthly audit. In January, April, July and October: the quarterly review and a refresh of your profile and pins |
| The 8th | **eToro long form** on Monday | Video episode on Thursday |
| The 15th | **Mid-month update** | Evergreen Question on Thursday |
| The 22nd | **eToro long form** on Monday | Video episode on Thursday. Next month's quotes drafted in one batch |

A Holding Anniversary lands on its own date. An earnings check lands after the print, never before.

## The year

| When | What |
|---|---|
| January | The annual letter. The Q4 review. Your plan for the year |
| April, July, October | The quarterly review |
| Earnings seasons | Earnings checks on your top holdings, after each print |
| Around an event | An event campaign: at least three picture posts and one short video, then a recap. Plan it two weeks ahead |

## Collision rules

1. **Promises never move.** When a promise and anything else want the same day, the other piece moves.
2. **One original per channel per slot.** Leave three hours between originals on X.
3. **At a quarter end** the month close ships first, the quarterly review after it.
4. **Re-check on the day.** A just-closed month is provisional for a day or two.
5. **An event week replaces the series**, it does not stack on them. The promises stay.
6. **Recurring posts change their wording every run.** Same question, new first line.
